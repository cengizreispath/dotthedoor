About

Every sentence ends with a dot. We like to think of it as a door.

Dot the Door is a small place to write the sentence that comes next: something you’re starting, deciding, letting go of, or don’t have a name for yet. Keep it, copy it, or leave it on the Dot Wall for others to read.

There are no accounts, likes or followers here. Just sentences, and the red dots people leave behind.

How the Dot Wall works

Every sentence is read by a filter before it goes up, and sometimes by a person. Links, contact details and anything that could hurt someone are held back.

See something that shouldn’t be here? Tap the sentence and choose Report. Changed your mind about your own? You can take your dot down from the device you posted it from, or write to info@dotthedoor.com.
